DiagraMinder is an independent personal project. For any privacy, access or deletion request: juanselopex55@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what the app needs. No advertising, no profiling, and we never sell or share your data.

Who we share it with

That is your choice and you can revoke the URL at any time from your profile. What that third party does with the data is governed by their policies, not this one.

Retention and your rights

Data is kept while your account exists. Email us and we will delete your account and its contents. You may request access, correction, export or deletion at any time, and you can revoke DiagraMinder's access from your Google account permissions.
